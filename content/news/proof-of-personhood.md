      [Proof of Personhood](https://agawen.com/art/personhood) (2024), XJ’s latest projection-mapped interactive installation, premiered at the Asian Art Museum’s Lee Gallery as a part of the museum’s [Research and Development](https://exhibitions.asianart.org/exhibitions/rad-research-and-development/) (RAD) program, curated by Cheng Xu. The [exhibition](https://calendar.asianart.org/event/proof-of-personhood-immersive-installation-and-artist-talk/) opened on October 6 with a fully reserved artist talk and audience Q&A.

      Borrowing its title from digital verification mechanisms that authenticates an online user as a real, unique human being, the installation responds to the “Dead Internet Theory”, the hypothesis that much of today’s internet is dominated by non-human activity. Inside the empty gallery, a lone spotlight decouples shadows from physical bodies, creating a space where visitors coexist with autonomous silhouettes that lack human origins, raising the question of what it means to live alongside AI-agents as digital shadows of ourselves.
