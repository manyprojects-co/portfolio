statement: >-
  About the CIRCA Prize: The CIRCA PRIZE is a global, annual initiative funded
  by the sale of #CIRCAECONOMY prints that invests in the future of art and
  culture by awarding £40,000 to the next generation of creative talent. The III
  edition of the prize unearthed over 1,000 films from across the planet, each a
  light shining amid the darkness in a constellation of collective belief.


  Thirty finalists were selected from over 20 countries including Ghana, India,
  Vietnam, Lithuania, Albania, France, the U.S. and the U.K. Throughout
  September, each finalist had the opportunity for their artwork to screen
  across the CIRCA global platform on Piccadilly Lights, London and in Berlin
  and Milan.
