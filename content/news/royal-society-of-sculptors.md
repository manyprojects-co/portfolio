      [BEAM](https://agawen.com/art/beam/) (2023) made its public debut at the
      [Royal Society of Sculptors](https://sculptors.org.uk/) as a finalist in
      the first edition of the [Interplay Kinetic Design
      Competition](https://www.designeducationtrust.org.uk/competition/).
      Co-developed with Wei Zhang and Luling Wang, the experimental kinetic lamp
      explores an alternative framework for domestic technology where objects
      are liberated from functional servitude and compliance.


      The work stands as a critique of a technological landscape that preys on
      consumers through addictive patterns and planned obsolescence. BEAM
      communicates through body language and light movement and is planned for a
      worn metal finish, proposing a more ambient technological interface that
      is built to patinate beautifully over time.


      BEAM was featured in the Dora House studio gallery from November 28
      through December 5, 2023.
