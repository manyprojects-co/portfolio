      From March 7 to March 19, 2026, XJ presented
      [Self—*ish*](https://helixartspace.se/previous-exhibitions/self-ish), the
      duo’s first international multi-work solo exhibition at [Helix Art
      Space](https://helixartspace.se/) in Stockholm, Sweden. Curated by Ruoxi
      Gao and co-developed with Yul Cho and Daniel Daboczy, the exhibition
      brought together five major works across kinetic sculpture, moving image,
      and video installations: [User](https://agawen.com/art/user) (2025),
      [Xin-1.0.36](https://agawen.com/art/xin-1036) (2026),
      [Screentime](https://agawen.com/art/screentime) (2024),
      [Metadata](https://agawen.com/art/metadata) (2026), and
      [Personhood](https://agawen.com/art/personhood) (2026 edition).


      “The exhibition traces how human habits and gestures are observed,
      recorded, and reconstructed within technological infrastructures. The
      title Self—ish captures this tension: it evokes the introspection of being
      alone with oneself, but also the way our personal gestures are always
      observed, recorded, and interpreted by external forces to grasp at an
      approximation of personhood. The infrastructures that promise agency are
      shaped by selfishness and technological determinism: forces that extract
      from the very selves they appear to empower” — Excerpt from exhibition
      program.


      The exhibition marked a major consolidation of XJ’s practice, which uses
      emerging technologies as artistic media to critically interrogate their
      consequences on the human condition.
