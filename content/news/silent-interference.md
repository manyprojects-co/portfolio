      [Non-moment](https://agawen.com/art/non-moment) (2025) premiered as part
      of [Silent Interference (靜噪)](https://www.turtle-chamber.com/), an outdoor
      group exhibition organized by [Turtle Chambers
      Projects](https://www.turtle-chamber.com/team) at the Fengling Commercial
      Building in Taoyuan, Taiwan. The exhibition featured works by 27
      international artists selected from over 150 submissions across 48
      countries and regions.


      Non-moment is a video work that isolates the micro-gestures of
      touch-screen interactions within a blank white screen. Viewed from the
      perspective of the device looking back at the user, the work reflects on
      unmade memories and unspent time lost to perpetual screen immersion.
