statement: >-
  Non-moment (2025) is a video art piece depicting the volumetric silhouette of
  a hand choreographed according to commonplace gestures of touch-screen
  interaction. It is a eulogy to the memories we will never make, eclipsed by
  the deluge of non-moments spent with our thumbs fixed on flat panels of light.


  The work was exhibited as a part of Turtle Chambers Projects' exhibition,
  Silent Interference (靜噪).
