      XJ** was awarded £3,000 in seed funding for a new artwork from [Kakilang](https://www.kakilang.org.uk/), a London-based non-profit arts organisation championing East and Southeast Asian (ESEA) artists and communities. They support work across art forms and empower creatives to develop ambitious, critically-engaged projects that are not reduced to racial identity.

      The proposed video sculpture uses satirical images of miniaturized home living inspired by social media parody accounts as a critique of rising living costs and constricting living standards. The work will debut as a part of Kakilang’s [2025 Autumn Season Program]([https://www.kakilang.org.uk/2025-autumn-season](https://www.kakilang.org.uk/2025-autumn-season)).
