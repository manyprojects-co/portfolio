    textContent: Premiering at the ++[ART-ACT
      Festival](https://sagg.info/event/art-act-festival-singapore/)++
      co-curated by Aura Murillo and Ina Conradi during Singapore Art Week 2023,
      ++[Box Body](https://agawen.com/art/box-body)++ (2023) was a site-specific
      anamorphic video installation that transformed the Ten Square LED
      billboard into a transparent volumetric container. Bypassing CGI
      animation, the installation utilized skewed live-action footage to create
      an optical anamorphic illusion, presenting a massive, crouched figure
      physically confined within the building’s facade. Serving to visually
      disrupt the regular commercial flow of the Selegie Art District, the
      artwork confronted passersby with a visceral metaphor for the pervasive
      sense of constriction that characterizes contemporary metropolitan life.

      medium of production by mixing performance art, illustration, 3D
      animation, photography, and videography. Together with the pieces, the
      artists presented a collection of art manifestos to reinforce their call
