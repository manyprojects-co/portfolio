      XJ inaugurated [Helix Art Space](https://helixartspace.se/)’s [Creative
      Breakfast
      series](https://has-creative-mornings-ai-creativity.confetti.events/) in
      Stockholm with an online talk exploring the role of emerging technologies
      within their art practice. Delivered remotely from London, the duo
      outlined their creative methodology regarding the deliberate treatment of
      artificial intelligence as a distinct artistic “material”, using recent
      works [2025PCM](https://agawen.com/art/2025pcm) (2025) and
      [Personhood](https://agawen.com/art/personhood) (2024) as comparative case
      studies to demonstrate deliberate usage or omission of computational
      tools.


      The talk serves as a prelude for XJ’s forthcoming solo exhibition at Helix
      Art Space, scheduled for March 2026.
