      A single-channel video adaptation of
      [Personhood](https://agawen.com/art/personhood/) (2024) was selected for
      the 17th edition of [Speculum Artium](https://speculumartium.si/wp21/), an
      annual festival in Slovenia dedicated to international new media art and
      contemporary techno-culture.


      This iteration of Personhood utilizes updated workflows to translate the
      work’s core inquiry into a moving-image format. Placing multiple
      silhouettes within the gallery to simulate plausible exhibition visitors,
      the work invites real visitors to reflect on what it means to share space
      with digital entities that convincingly mimic our presence.
