  - textContent: |-
      A [first-cut screening](https://bowarts.org/event/bow-skills-talk-screening-an-act-of-homemaking-with-esea-artist-duo-xj-in-collaboration-with-kakilang/) of the video sculpture [2025PCM](https://agawen.com/art/2025pcm) (2025) was presented at Bow Arts in London, fulfilling a commission by contemporary arts organization [Kakilang](https://www.kakilang.org.uk/). The presentation was accompanied by a panel discussion with [Howl Yuan](https://howlyuan.com/) (Artistic Director, [Kakilang](https://www.kakilang.org.uk/)) and Leah Jun Oh ([Bow Arts](https://bowarts.org/)), addressing the intersections between London’s housing crisis, art studio shortages, and the structural challenges faced by ESEA and migrant artists.

      Designed for 90-degree building facades, 2025PCM combines physical performance and architectural projection mapping to depict a hyper-compressed, one-cubic-meter flat fully furnished for living. Rooted in lived experiences of rising living costs and constant relocation, the short film uses absurdity to examine the destabilizing financialization of urban life and particularly its effects on the individual.

      [Bow Skills talk & screening](https://bowarts.org/event/bow-skills-talk-screening-an-act-of-homemaking-with-esea-artist-duo-xj-in-collaboration-with-kakilang/): an act of homemaking with ESEA artist duo X&J in collaboration with Kakilang.  

      Join us for a conversation with artist duo XJ (Xin Wen & JJ Agcaoili) in collaboration with Kakilang, a UK-based contemporary arts organisation that champions East and Southeast Asian voices. X&J will present their new work 2,025 PCM for the first time and share their lived experiences as being migrant creatives in London. They will be joined by Howl Yuan at Kakilang and Leah Jun Oh at Bow Arts to share insights on both organisations’ work, particularly concerning the intersection of housing crises, art studio shortages and the challenges faced by migrant artists.
