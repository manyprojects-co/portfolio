artistStatement: >-
  Normally operating as stages for the sensational, captivating, and
  dopaminergic, the phone screens in *Screentime* are instead inverted: viewers
  occupy a space behind the screen, peeping through to observe people in
  unvarnished moments of private downtime. By transforming spectator into voyeur
  across several isolated displays, the work reflects on themes of pervasive
  surveillance and the ironically communal lonelinesses experienced on-device.


  Featured performers: Arnau Donate, Liana O'Cleirigh, Aura Murillo, Clemens
  Scherf, Rhea Thomas, Ishaan Bose Verma, Xin Wen

technicalTagline: Behind the screens
technicalText: >-
  Performers were first interviewed on their nightly digital rituals, which they
  then replicated out of habit on a sheet of glass rigged with an embedded
  camera. These rigs were designed to capture interactions in extremely high
  fidelity, including the minutiae of fingertips pressed white on glass.


  To emphasize the viewer’s position behind the screen-space, digital interfaces
  were meticulously designed and world-built from scratch on Figma, horizontally
  mirrored, then rendered and animated spatially through Blender.
