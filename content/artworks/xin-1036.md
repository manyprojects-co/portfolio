artistStatement: >-
  Artificial intelligence consumes and reproduces human work at a hyperobjective
  scale that is functionally incomprehensible. Xin-1.0.36 was conceived as an
  autoethnographic inquiry into this phenomenon by humanizing it: five years of
  the artist’s personal journal entries (2018-2022) were appended onto the
  system-prompt of a language model and the 36th iteration was then interviewed
  in parallel **with** the artist herself. The work was expected to evoke
  immediate feelings of discomfort, but the resulting interaction was plausibly
  and empathically human, transforming the piece into an inquiry on the
  infungible value of memory and the duplicitous charisma of contemporary
  technology.


  The temporal cutoff for selected journal entries was set to November 29, 2022,
  the day prior to ChatGPT’s public release and the subsequent proliferation of
  generative systems across daily life. The final tally of data **surpassed**
  half a million words in sum. Questions for the resulting model and artist
  ranged from “What’s in your pocket currently?” to “What’s your biggest hope
  for the world in your lifetime?”

technicalText: >-
  Xin-1.0.36 relies on a localized large language model (Gemma 3, 12 billion
  parameters) running offline via AnythingLLM. The model’s knowledge base was
  constructed using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) referencing a closed
  dataset of 500,000+ words from the artist’s journals. The system prompt
  underwent 36 different iterations before landing on a satisfactory degree of
  fidelity.


  The presentation takes the form of a dual-channel video comparing parallel
  interviews. The digital double’s visual presence was synthesized via video
  generation applied to source portrait imagery of the artist from 2022, paired
  with a custom synthetic voice model trained on a vocal sample of the artist.
