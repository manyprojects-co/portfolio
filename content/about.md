bio: >-
  Agawen is the studio practice of J. Aga (PH) and Xin Wen (CN/USA). Their
  collaboration began in London, where they completed a joint postgraduate
  programme between the Royal College of Art and Imperial College London. They
  made their artistic debut during a term abroad in Singapore, followed by a UK
  debut later that year on the Piccadilly Lights, which received the CIRCA
  Public Vote Prize.


  Their current body of work is about how contemporary life is structurally
  determined by cities and screens, and it draws from their experience as
  diasporic artists and their technical foundation as design-engineers. Their
  experimental videos, kinetic sculptures, and immersive installations have been
  exhibited at the Asian Art Museum (San Francisco, USA), Ars Electronica (Linz,
  Austria), Helix Art Space (Stockholm, Sweden), and Speculum Artium (Trbovlje,
  Slovenia).


  *Formerly XJ.*
